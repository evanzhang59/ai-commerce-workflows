# Derived asset acceptance and bounded handoff

This Skill remains an identity, audit and staging workflow. It does not edit source pixels or grant upload authority. Use the available image workflow for a separately authorized edit, following its tool and method requirements. Do not silently switch editing methods to satisfy a failed fidelity check.

Evaluate identity, channel technical rules, visual fidelity and production authorization separately. An official source is not sufficient variant evidence, and a technically compliant file is not automatically approved. Keep exact source identifiers, provenance and immutable original. Never infer a shade from a multicolor image or use product-level fallback beyond its approved channel, population and asset role.

Treat generated or edited derivatives as REVIEW until the agreed identity/fidelity checks and production approval are recorded. Store derivative provenance, original hash, edit scope, permitted crop/canvas change, protected regions and approval reference alongside the existing asset manifest; do not silently change the manifest's required schema or READY validator.

For an explicitly strict text-only edit, retain the approved original canvas/crop and require the specified non-target preservation evidence. If the contract requires unchanged pixels outside a mask, verify that property against the aligned original, with no undeclared resampling or lossy transformation used to hide differences. Visual review must also confirm packaging, shape, shade and composition. A generative output that changes these fails the contract even if the text is gone. If available tools cannot meet the contract, disclose the limitation and request a method/scope decision; never relabel the altered output as faithful.

Hand off only the verified, approved derivative and its bounded use permission. Upload/publication belongs to the authorized channel owner. Preserve rejected attempts and do not convert a corrected asset into a claim of first-pass success.
